Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our milk quality, bottles, and billing.

Why does Panditji use glass instead of standard plastic pouches? +
Warm milk packed in polyethylene pouches leaches phthalates and microplastics into the fat globules. Glass is chemically inert, non-porous, and preserves the authentic taste and thick malai layer without any toxic migration.
How does the bottle return deduction reflect on my bill? +
When you return your clean bottle each morning, our delivery partner scans its barcoded neck. ₹20 (1000ml), ₹10 (500ml), or ₹5 (250ml) is immediately logged in your digital ledger. This reduces your monthly UPI AutoPay deduction by up to ₹600/month.
Can I pause milk delivery if I go out of town? +
Yes! You can pause delivery anytime directly from WhatsApp or our customer web portal before 9:00 PM the previous evening. Any paused days carry over to extend your subscription validity automatically.
Is your buffalo milk pasteurized or raw? +
We gently low-temperature pasteurize our milk to retain natural lactoferrin and digestive enzymes while destroying harmful pathogens, then chill it immediately to 4°C for dawn dispatch.
